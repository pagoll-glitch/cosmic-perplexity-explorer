Mathematical Calculation of Perplexity
$$\text{PPL} = \exp\left( -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln(P(\text{token}_i \mid \text{previous tokens})) \right) = \left( \prod_{i=1}^N \frac{1}{P(\text{token}_i \mid \text{previous tokens})} \right)^{\frac{1}{N}}$$
Perplexity is the geometric mean of the reciprocal probabilities across all evaluated tokens. Lower perplexity indicates that the language model assigned higher probability to your words, finding the sequence more predictable and natural.

The Mathematics Behind Language Models

Connecting neural network logits, softmax normalization, surprisal, and perplexity.

01. From Logits to Probabilities

The language model outputs an unnormalized score (logit $z_i$) for each of the 50,257 tokens in its vocabulary. The Softmax function exponentiates and normalizes these logits into a valid probability distribution summing strictly to 1:

$P(t_i) = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^V e^{z_j}}$

02. Surprisal & Information Theory

In Claude Shannon's Information Theory, an event with probability $P$ carries a surprisal (or self-information) of:

$I(x) = -\ln(P(x)) \text{ nats} = -\log_2(P(x)) \text{ bits}$

If a token is certain ($P = 0.99$), surprisal is near zero. If a token is rare ($P = 0.0001$), surprisal surges. Perplexity is mathematically the exponentiated average surprisal.

03. The N-Sided Die Metaphor

Imagine guessing the next word. If perplexity is 8.0, the model was as uncertain as someone choosing uniformly at random from an 8-sided die.

A perplexity of 1.0 represents total certainty. Random guessing over DistilGPT-2's vocabulary would yield a perplexity of 50,257!

The Mathematics of Language Modeling

A causal autoregressive language model decomposes the joint probability of a sequence of tokens $W = (w_1, w_2, \dots, w_N)$ using the probability chain rule:

$P(W) = \prod_{i=1}^N P(w_i \mid w_1, w_2, \dots, w_{i-1})$

Starting-Token Convention

Autoregressive models predict token $i$ based on tokens $1 \dots i-1$. But what predicts token 1?

  • Conditioned on BOS (Default): The model prefixes the standard start-of-sequence token <|endoftext|> (token 50256 in DistilGPT-2), allowing it to assign a proper conditional probability $P(w_1 \mid \text{BOS})$ to the opening word.
  • Context Only: Token 1 is treated as given prompt context without scoring, and perplexity is evaluated solely over transitions $w_2 \dots w_N$ ($N-1$ tokens).

Why Geometric Mean Instead of Arithmetic Mean?

Probabilities multiply; they do not add. If two tokens have probabilities 0.9 and 0.0001, their arithmetic mean is ~0.45 (deceivingly optimistic). The geometric mean properly penalizes the catastrophic failure on the second token, revealing that the model was severely perplexed.
